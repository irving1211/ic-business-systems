Help with a suspended Google Business Profile in Lawrence, MA

If your Google Business Profile was suspended, I find what likely triggered it, fix the profile so it follows Google's guidelines, and file the appeal with you, with the right proof. I'm Irving Carrion in Lawrence, MA, and I work with real local businesses anywhere in the US.

By Irving Carrion · Updated October 27, 2026

What's included.

Most suspensions come from something on the profile that breaks Google's rules: a name with extra keywords, an address problem, the wrong category or a duplicate listing. I've worked through Google's profile ownership and access recovery steps for local business owners.

  • Finding the likely cause: name, address, service area, category, duplicates and recent edits
  • Fixing the profile so it follows Google's guidelines
  • A list of exactly which documents and photos Google is likely to ask for
  • Writing and filing the appeal with you, step by step
  • Checking the status and telling you what to do next
  • If an appeal was already denied: a review of what was missed and a stronger second appeal

Who it's for.

Real businesses with a real address or service area and proof: a business license, a utility bill, signage, or photos of your work vehicle and tools. I don't help fake or virtual-office listings.

Honest limits.

  • Google decides every appeal. No one can promise reinstatement. I give your appeal its best shot.
  • Real businesses only. I don't help fake or virtual-office listings.
  • Google often answers in a few days, sometimes a few weeks. What I deliver is the fix and the filed appeal.

How it works.

  1. Message me first

    Send a screenshot of the suspension message and tell me what you changed on the profile before it happened. I'll tell you whether it's a suspension or just an unverified profile.

  2. Fix it and gather proof

    I fix what likely caused it and tell you exactly which documents and photos fit your case.

  3. File the appeal

    We file the appeal together, step by step. Then I check the status with you and tell you what to do next.

Common questions.

Can you guarantee my profile comes back?

No. Google decides. What I can do is remove what likely caused the suspension and send an appeal with the proof Google asks for.

How long does Google take?

Often a few days, sometimes a few weeks. I check the status with you after the appeal is filed.

What proof will I need?

Usually a business license or registration, a utility bill or lease, and photos of signage, your vehicle or your work. I tell you exactly what fits your case.

Do you need my password?

No. You sign in and share your screen on a video call, or you follow my steps. I never ask for your password.

Should I make a new profile instead?

No. A second profile for the same business usually makes things worse. Fix and appeal the original.

What it costs.

Every case is different, so I look at your situation first and quote it in writing before any work starts. If it turns out you only need verification, I'll tell you before you pay for an appeal.
