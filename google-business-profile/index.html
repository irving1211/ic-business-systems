Google Business Profile setup for trades in Lawrence, MA

What's included.

Most calls for plumbers, HVAC and other trades start on Google Maps. I've worked in the plumbing trade, so I write services the way customers search for them. I built the website and updated the Google profile for a local plumbing company.

  • The right main category and extra categories
  • A description and a service list for every job you do
  • Service area, hours and contact details that match your website
  • Your real job photos, uploaded and organized
  • Google posts written for you
  • A review-request link and a short text to send happy customers

Who it's for.

Plumbers, HVAC companies, electricians and other trades with a real Google Business Profile that's thin, out of date or doesn't match the website. If your profile is suspended, start with the suspended profile service instead.

Honest limits.

  • No one can promise a top-3 spot on Google Maps. Distance, reviews and competition decide most of it.
  • No fake reviews, no keyword-stuffed business names and no “geotag” tricks. They break Google's rules or don't work. I fix what you control.
  • Edits usually show within a few days, once Google reviews them. Any change in where you show up takes weeks.

How it works.

  1. Add me as a Manager

    You add me in your profile's People and access settings. I send the steps. You stay the owner and can remove me any time.

  2. I make the changes

    Categories, services, service area, hours, photos and posts, all matched to your website and the towns you cover.

  3. You get a list of every change

    Every edit is written down, along with your review link and the text to send customers.

Common questions.

Do I have to give you my password?

No. You add me as a Manager in your profile's People and access settings. You stay the owner and can remove me any time.

Can you get me more reviews?

I give you a review link and a short text to send happy customers. I never write or buy reviews. That breaks Google's rules and can get a profile suspended.

Do you geotag my photos?

No. Google removes location data from uploaded photos, so geotagging doesn't help. I upload real job photos with clear names and captions instead.

My profile is suspended or not verified. Can you help?

Yes. A suspended profile needs a fix and an appeal, which I handle as its own service. For verification, Google checks with you directly, usually by video, and I walk you through it.

Do you only work with Massachusetts businesses?

No. I'm based in Lawrence, MA and meet owners in person across the Merrimack Valley and southern New Hampshire, and I work with trades businesses anywhere in the US.
