Questions owners ask first.

By Irving Carrion · Updated October 29, 2026

Is the review really free?

Yes. You get the video and the written fixes either way. There's no obligation and no sales sequence afterward.

I'm already on Squarespace or Wix. Do I have to switch?

No. If your platform works, I'll improve what you have. I only recommend moving when the platform is the thing costing you jobs.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, if you want to. I can build it on a platform you can edit, or handle edits for you on a monthly plan.

Do you work in Spanish?

Sí. I build sites in English, Spanish, or both, and we can talk in whichever you prefer.

Do you only work with local businesses?

No. I'm based in Lawrence and meet clients in person across Massachusetts and New Hampshire. I also build for businesses anywhere, working remotely.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. Your domain and accounts stay in your name, and I hand everything over at launch.

How long does it take?

Most websites take one to four weeks once I have your content and access. Bigger systems take longer, and you get the date in writing.

What's the difference between the free review and paid consulting?

The free review is me looking at your site from the outside, the way a customer does, then sending a short video with your three most valuable fixes. Paid consulting is hands-on: I get into your website or tools, find what's wrong and fix it, at $75 an hour with the time agreed first.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of fixes are included. After that it's up to you: monthly website support from $150 a month, or consulting at $75 an hour only when you need something.

Are hosting and software included?

No. Hosting, domains and software subscriptions stay in your name and you pay those companies directly. My prices cover my time and work.

Is there a contract for monthly plans?

No long contract. Monthly plans are month to month, and you can cancel any time before your next billing date.

Who do you build websites for, and where?

Mostly plumbers, HVAC companies and other trades, plus other owner-led local businesses. I'm based in Lawrence, MA and meet clients in person across the Merrimack Valley (Methuen, Andover, North Andover, Haverhill, Lowell) and southern New Hampshire. I also work remotely with businesses anywhere.

Is IC Business Systems the same as IC System?

No. IC Business Systems is my web design business in Lawrence, MA. It has no connection to IC System, the debt collection company.
