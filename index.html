Frequently asked questions

The questions most owners ask before they reach out.

If you are not a technical person, that is fine. This should still make sense.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Any small business, but this is especially strong for local service businesses, trades, owner-operators, and businesses that need better follow-up and a stronger online presence.

Do I need a brand new website?

Not always. Sometimes the best move is a cleaner website. Sometimes it is fixing contact flow, follow-up, or how leads are being handled. The point is solving the real problem, not forcing a rebuild.

What does business automation actually mean?

It means setting up simple systems that save time and reduce missed opportunities. That could be lead follow-up, inquiry routing, reminders, intake, or repetitive admin work that keeps slowing the business down.

Do I need to understand any of the tech?

No. The whole point is to make this easier to use, not harder to understand. The work should feel clear on your side and useful in the real business.

Can you help bilingual businesses too?

Yes. If your customers move between English and Spanish, the website and customer flow can be built to support that more naturally.

How much does this usually cost?

It depends on what actually needs to be fixed. Some businesses need a cleaner website. Some need follow-up and intake fixed first. The quote form helps me see the real problem so I can recommend the right scope instead of guessing.

How long does this usually take?

Smaller fixes can move fast. Bigger builds take longer. The first step is figuring out whether you need one clean fix or a fuller system build.
